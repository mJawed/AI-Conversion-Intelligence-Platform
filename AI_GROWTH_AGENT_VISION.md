# AI Growth Agent

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Vision Document

**Mission:**
Build the world's first AI-powered Growth Agent that continuously observes visitor behaviour, understands why users do or don't convert, predicts future outcomes, and safely takes actions to improve website revenue.

---

# Vision

Most analytics platforms answer:

> **What happened?**

Our platform answers:

- Why did it happen?
- What should I do?
- What will happen next?
- Can AI fix it automatically?

The long-term vision is to build an AI teammate that works 24/7 to improve a company's website without requiring someone to manually analyse dashboards.

Instead of selling an analytics platform, we are building an **AI Growth Employee**.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The final product is not an analytics dashboard.

It is an AI Chief Growth Officer.

A business owner installs one tracking script.

The AI then

- Watches every visitor
- Understands every journey
- Detects every problem
- Explains every issue
- Prioritises improvements
- Predicts outcomes
- Runs experiments
- Executes approved actions
- Learns continuously
- Reports business impact

The owner no longer spends time analysing dashboards.

Instead, they simply ask:

"What should we improve today?"

The AI answers with evidence, expected business impact, and—when authorised—takes the necessary actions automatically.
